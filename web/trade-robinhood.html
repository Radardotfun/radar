Fees and checks

Radar takes no fee. You pay the Uniswap pool's fee and Robinhood Chain's network fee. Some coins take heavy fees or taxes, so before your wallet sees the buy, Radar simulates it and a sale right after it. It stops the buy if it would lose more than 10%, if buying and selling back would lose more than 20%, or if the sale fails. Read the checks
