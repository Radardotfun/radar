Mobile test cases

For the Microsoft Edge Android and iOS QA team. Extension ID lchjfepnoaglhmnbbjffhbmfbchnojml. No account or sign-in is needed for Radar itself.

Setup

  • Microsoft Edge for Android or iOS, current stable or Canary, with Radar installed from its Edge Add-ons listing.
  • Open x.com in Edge, not the X app. Post pages can be viewed without signing in to X.
  • Sample narrative post: https://x.com/cb_doge/status/2112923907644928395
  • Sample post with a contract address: any recent result of the X search pump ca (Latest tab).

TC1. Radar button appears under posts

Steps: open the sample narrative post. Scroll the timeline or the replies.

Expected: each post's action bar (reply, repost, like, bookmark, share) has an extra radar icon. Opening x.com for the first time shows a one-time tip, "Tap to see the coins behind this post", with a Got it button.

TC2. Scan a post

Steps: tap the radar icon under the sample narrative post.

Expected: a panel opens over the page within a second. It shows the post's author and text, then a one-line narrative (for the sample: Lufthansa adopting Starlink). Within about 10 seconds, up to three coin cards appear, labelled Top market cap, The OG and The mover. Each shows a name, ticker, shortened contract address, market cap, age and 24h volume.

TC3. Expand a coin card

Steps: tap the arrow on the first card.

Expected: the card expands to show liquidity, holders, 24h transactions, Bundled supply (a percentage, or a short note that it could not be read), a Launch audit (top 10 holders, mint/freeze, LP, RugCheck), and buttons for Axiom, Padre, Fomo, GMGN, Dex, Solscan, RugCheck and Open Bubblemaps.

TC4. Copy the contract address

Steps: tap the copy icon next to the shortened address on any card.

Expected: a short "Copied" confirmation. Pasting elsewhere gives the full address.

TC5. Open a trading or chart link

Steps: on an expanded card, tap Dex, then go back. Tap Open Bubblemaps.

Expected: each opens the coin's page on that site in a new tab. x.com stays open with the panel.

TC6. Contract address shown under a post

Steps: open the X search pump ca, Latest tab, and scroll to a post that contains a Solana contract address.

Expected: within a few seconds a small line appears under that post with the coin's ticker, market cap and age (for example "$COIN $80.6K · 28m"). Tapping it opens the panel on that post.

TC7. Live refresh

Steps: keep the panel open for 30 seconds.

Expected: the footer reads "updated Ns ago" and resets about every 10 seconds; numbers on the cards update in place.

TC8. Open and close the panel

Steps: close the panel with its X button. Tap the round radar button floating at the bottom right of the page.

Expected: the panel closes, then reopens showing the last scan. The floating button does not cover X's own post (compose) button.

Notes

  • Data comes from DexScreener, RugCheck and the Solana network, read live; results depend on what is trading at test time.
  • A post with no coin narrative (for example a personal update) shows "No coin narrative here". That is expected behaviour, not a failure.
